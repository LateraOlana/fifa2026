How results are counted

Group matches are checked against the original pre-match pick: Team A win, draw, or Team B win. Knockout matches are checked by the team that advances when the scoreboard source identifies a winner. The predicted non-winner metric excludes matches where the original pick was a draw. If the original pick selected one team to win, the other team is the predicted non-winner; the check is correct when that team does not win. Matches that are not final yet are not included.
